Curation methodology

Scope, evidence and quality controls

The public methods layer emphasizes scientific semantics, data grain and reproducible decision rules.

01

Article identity

One article-audit row per normalized DOI. Compounds, phases, polymorphs and temperature determinations are expanded separately.

02

Chemical scope

Organic-containing Cu(I) chloride, bromide and iodide systems, with compound-level adjudication rather than title-only screening.

03

Evidence hierarchy

CIF → SI crystallographic table → main article → external bibliographic metadata. Missing crystallography is never inferred from analogy.

04

Structure mapping

Formula/label, phase/temperature, canonical space group, mapping confidence and determination method are preserved at structure grain.

05

Polarity

Polarity is derived from crystallographic point group. Non-centrosymmetric, polar and ferroelectric are distinct concepts.

06

Evidence grain

Article-level photophysics is not assigned to an individual structure/phase unless a structure-grain evidence mapping establishes that link.

Evidence and access model

Public scientific interface

Selected bibliographic, structural and crystallographic fields are exposed through server-side, field-whitelisted projection queries and single-record views. The goal is scientific discoverability and traceability, not bulk redistribution.

Private provenance layer

Primary PDFs, SI, CIF payloads, evidence excerpts/locators, full field-evidence objects, QA logs, candidate-screening rationale and complete normalized tables remain private research assets.

Smart RAG runtime boundary

The current runtime combines deterministic scientific guards, evidence retrieval and a bounded claim-validation layer. Provider degradation is reported explicitly rather than hidden. The legacy 70/70 benchmark predates the final v9 orchestration and is not represented as a fresh current-runtime benchmark.

Known release erratum

Four Record 13 structure-dimensionality cells inherited a list-position mapping error. The immutable 3.0.1 archive remains unchanged; effective public values are corrected, with formal snapshot correction reserved for 3.0.2.
