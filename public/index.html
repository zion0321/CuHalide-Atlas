Curation methodology

Scope, evidence and quality controls

The public methods page emphasizes scientific semantics and reproducible decision rules. Implementation details are secondary to the evidence boundary.

01

Article identity

One row per normalized DOI. Compound, phase, polymorph and temperature determinations are expanded separately at structure grain.

02

Chemical scope

Organic-containing Cu(I) chloride, bromide and iodide systems, with compound-level inclusion/exclusion rather than title-only screening.

03

Evidence hierarchy

CIF → SI crystallographic table → main article → external bibliographic metadata. Space groups are never inferred from formula or analogy.

04

Structure mapping

Formula/label, phase/temperature, canonical space group, mapping confidence and determination method are preserved at structure grain.

05

Polarity

Polarity is derived from crystallographic point group. Non-centrosymmetric, polar and ferroelectric are treated as distinct concepts.

06

Missingness & QA

Unreported, unavailable and unresolved values remain explicit. Release gates recompute key counts, subset membership and evidence invariants.

Evidence and access model

Public scientific interface

The website exposes selected bibliographic, structural, crystallographic and photophysical fields through server-side query and record views. The aim is scientific discoverability and traceability, not bulk redistribution of the complete normalized curation corpus.

Private provenance layer

Primary article PDFs, SI, CIF payloads, evidence excerpts/locators, full field-evidence objects, QA logs, candidate screening rationale and complete normalized tables remain private research assets.

Smart RAG technical implementation

The production system combines deterministic exact/property/boundary routes with precision/full-text/semantic retrieval and reranking. Bounded LLM interpretation is used only when requested and is subject to source-level claim validation. When the external AI allocation is unavailable, the service falls back to deterministic/precision retrieval rather than inventing unsupported synthesis.

Known release erratum

Four Record 13 structure-dimensionality cells inherited a list-position mapping error. The immutable 3.0.1 archive remains unchanged; the public effective display is corrected, and formal scientific correction is reserved for 3.0.2. Article counts, space-group counts, polar subsets and canonical denominators are unaffected.

Citation & data availability

Use and cite CuHalide Atlas

The public portal is designed for query, inspection and evidence-grounded scientific use. The complete internal curation corpus is not distributed as a public bulk download.

Recommended citation

CuHalide Atlas. Release 3.0.1 (10 August 2026). https://cuhalide-atlas-v3.vercel.app/
Citation metadata

Include an access date if required by the target journal. Consult the public known-errata notice when reporting Record 13 dimensionality.
