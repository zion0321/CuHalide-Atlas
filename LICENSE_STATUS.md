# License status

No top-level open-source or open-data license has been approved for CuHalide Atlas through **Frozen Release 3.0.2** and **Current Curated rev.7**. The repository and review portal are currently in a **prepublication review state** and do not assign a new license on behalf of the rights holder.

Before permanent DOI deposition, formal public release, or any blanket reuse statement, the repository owner must review and approve licenses appropriate to:

- original software and workflow code;
- original curated database fields and documentation;
- third-party bibliographic metadata;
- figures and other potentially copyrighted material.

Primary articles, supporting information and CIF files are provenance inputs and are not redistributed through the review portal. Complete normalized tables, raw evidence/QA assets and other private curation materials are also not offered as a public bulk release.

Absence of a declared license must not be interpreted as permission to reuse or redistribute copyrighted third-party content. Machine-readable archival/deposition metadata uses `NOASSERTION` until an owner-authorized rights decision is complete. The current repository metadata does not assert a permanent DOI or formal public-release date.
